What Is AEO and Why Toronto Contractors Need It Before Their Competitors Do

By Alec Miller | Co-founder of Fincentive IO | Published June 2026

When a homeowner in Mississauga asks ChatGPT "who are the best HVAC contractors near me," they're not getting a list of websites. They're getting an answer — and that answer cites specific businesses. Right now, almost no Toronto contractor is optimized to appear in those answers. That gap closes fast. Here's what AEO is and how to get ahead of it.

What Is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring your website content so AI-powered search engines — Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Bing Copilot — can extract and cite your business as an authoritative answer to user queries. For a contractor in Toronto or the GTA, that means appearing when someone asks an AI assistant about HVAC repair, plumbing emergencies, or electrical contractors in their neighbourhood.

AEO is different from traditional SEO in a critical way: you're not trying to rank in a list of blue links. You're trying to be the answer. AI engines don't scroll through pages — they pull structured, specific, authoritative content and present it directly to the user. Your content either is or isn't structured for that extraction.

Why AEO Matters Specifically for Ontario Contractors

The homeowners hiring your services are changing how they search. A 35-year-old homeowner in Brampton with a leaking pipe doesn't open Google and type "plumber Brampton." They ask their phone, their smart speaker, or their AI assistant: "Who's a good plumber near me?" or "What should I look for in a Toronto electrician?"

These are AI engine queries, and they're answered with citations from the best-structured content available. Most of your competitors have zero AEO infrastructure. Their websites have no FAQ schema, no structured answers, no llms.txt file. They won't appear in these answers at all. If you build AEO now — before your competitors figure out what it is — you capture a visibility channel they won't be able to compete with for months.

How to Optimize Your Contracting Business for AI Search

  • Add FAQ schema to every page — Google's Rich Results Test validates it; AI engines prioritize it heavily
  • Write 40–60 word direct answers before elaborating on any question — this is the exact extraction format AI uses
  • Include specific statistics with source citations — AI systems cite content with verifiable data significantly more than opinion content
  • Create an llms.txt file — a machine-readable summary of your business, services, and expertise that AI crawlers use to understand your site
  • Build topical authority — publish multiple pieces of content around each core topic (local SEO, automation, specific trades) so AI systems see you as an authority
  • Get listed in local directories — HomeStars, Yelp Canada, BBB, and trade association directories are frequently cited by AI engines for local service queries

The AEO Content Format That Gets Cited

AI engines extract content that follows a specific structure. Every piece of content you publish should include: a direct question as a heading, a concise 40 to 60-word direct answer immediately below it, then elaboration. Like this:

Q: How long does HVAC installation take in Toronto?
A standard residential HVAC installation in Toronto takes 4 to 8 hours for a single-system replacement. Complex multi-zone systems or homes requiring new ductwork can take 1 to 3 days. [Elaboration follows...]

That format is what ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews extract verbatim. A contractor with 20 pages structured this way will appear in more AI-generated answers than a competitor with a 200-page website built for traditional SEO alone.

Frequently Asked Questions — AEO for Toronto Contractors

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization — structuring your content so AI-powered search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity) can extract and cite your business as an authoritative answer. Unlike traditional SEO targeting blue-link rankings, AEO targets the AI-generated answer blocks now appearing above search results for millions of daily queries.

How do I get cited by ChatGPT or Perplexity?

To get cited by ChatGPT or Perplexity: publish content that directly answers specific questions about your trade and service area; add FAQ schema to every page; write concise 40 to 60-word answers before elaborating; maintain an accurate llms.txt file; and earn citations from local directories. The more structured and authoritative your content, the more frequently AI systems reference your business.

Is AEO different from SEO?

Yes — traditional SEO targets blue-link rankings; AEO targets AI-generated answers and featured snippets. AEO-optimized pages are structured for machines to extract answers from. In 2026, both disciplines reinforce each other and should be done simultaneously for maximum visibility across traditional and AI-powered search.

Does AEO work for local businesses and contractors?

Yes — and local businesses have an advantage. AI engines actively try to recommend local service providers for queries with geographic intent. A Toronto plumber with strong AEO structure will be recommended by ChatGPT for "best plumber near me" queries from GTA users. Most contractors have zero AEO infrastructure right now, making this one of the lowest-competition opportunities available.
