What Is Contractor Lead Generation?

Contractor lead generation is the systematic process of attracting homeowners, property managers, and businesses who need your services and converting them into booked jobs. For trades businesses in Toronto and Ontario, this means showing up in Google Maps, ranking in search results, running targeted Google and Meta ads, and having automated systems that follow up with every inquiry immediately — before the customer moves on to the next contractor they found.

Effective lead generation isn't a single tactic. The contractors generating consistent, predictable lead flow are running at least two or three channels simultaneously and following up fast. That's exactly the system Fincentive IO builds.
