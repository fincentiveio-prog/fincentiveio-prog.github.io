Our Story

We Help Businesses Grow Smarter With AI, SEO & Strategy

Fincentive IO was built for the business owners who are too busy doing the work to step back and figure out how to grow it. We bring the strategy, the systems, and the execution — so you don't have to figure it all out alone.

Our Story

Built by People Who've Been in the Trenches

Fincentive IO was founded in Toronto with one clear purpose: to give contractors, trades professionals, and growing businesses access to the same calibre of strategic support that large corporations take for granted — without the agency overhead, the vague deliverables, or the monthly reports that don't connect to anything real.

We started by working directly with trades business owners who were generating real revenue but couldn't figure out why they weren't keeping more of it. The answers were almost always the same: no system for generating consistent leads, too much time spent on admin, and no clear strategy for what to do next. We built our service model around solving exactly those problems — and we've been refining it ever since.

Today, Fincentive IO operates at the intersection of AI automation, local SEO, digital advertising, and business consulting. We work with clients across Toronto and Canada — from sole-operator plumbers to multi-location service companies — and our results speak for themselves.

Our Mission

"To make world-class business strategy accessible to every contractor, tradesperson, and entrepreneur — and to actually help them execute it."

We believe the gap between knowing what to do and doing it is where most businesses lose. Fincentive IO exists to close that gap — with hands-on support, smart tools, and a team that stays with you through execution, not just planning.
