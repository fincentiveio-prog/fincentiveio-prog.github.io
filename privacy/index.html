Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 30, 2026 Company: Fincentive Corp. Location: 637-33 Bloor St E, Toronto, ON, Canada, M4W 3H1

At Fincentive Corp. ("we," "our," or "us"), your privacy is of utmost importance. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or utilize our services. By accessing our website, you agree to the practices described herein.

1. Data Collection

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Includes your name, email address, phone number, business details, and other information you voluntarily provide.
  • Technical Information: Includes IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and analytics data to enhance your experience on our website.
  • Business Data: Information related to your business operations that you share with us for consulting or analytical purposes.

2. Data Usage

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • To deliver customized solutions and services tailored to your business needs.
  • To improve our website's functionality and user experience.
  • To communicate with you about updates, services, and support.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and conditions.

3. Data Storage and Confidentiality

All data acquired by Fincentive Corp. will remain strictly confidential and securely stored on our private cloud server. We do not share, sell, or rent your data to third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law.

We employ advanced security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include:

  • Encrypted data transmission protocols.
  • Regular vulnerability assessments and updates.
  • Role-based access controls to ensure only authorized personnel access sensitive information.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. Upon request, we will delete your personal information in accordance with applicable regulations.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information held by us.
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request the removal of your data from our systems, subject to legal and contractual obligations.
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data under certain circumstances.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@fincentive.io.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may include links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies to understand how they handle your data.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze website traffic. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy as needed to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Effective Date" will be updated accordingly.
